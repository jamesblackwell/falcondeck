# Console submission

Checked 5 October 2026.

Send the draft to **hello@console.dev**. Console's selection page asks for an email with tool details; there is no prescribed form, subject, attachment or word count.

They look for tools developers can try themselves and use regularly, with good installation, maintenance and docs. The draft supplies download/source/quickstart links and explains a real development workflow. Mac-only desktop availability and existing agent costs are stated, rather than implying broad platform coverage or free model usage.

Console also lists pre-1.0/labelled beta releases. FalconDeck's current 0.1.x releases fit that version criterion, but this is an editorial suggestion, not a promise of inclusion. The email leaves the appropriate section to the editor. Do not send an advertising enquiry; sponsorship is separate from editorial reviews.

No attachment is needed. Send from your normal email and keep the James signature. If asked for screenshots, use the current site or a real task with private code hidden.

Source: [selection criteria and submission address](https://console.dev/selection-criteria).
